## Exact skill boundaries and examples

### P1-COO-01 — Equation of a straight line
Canonical action: form a line equation from a point + gradient or two points.
Example:
point (2,3), gradient 4
y - 3 = 4(x - 2)
y = 4x - 5.
If two points are given, find the gradient first, then use point-gradient form.
Exclude parallel/perpendicular conditions as the main teaching target.

### P1-COO-03 — Parallel and perpendicular lines
Anchor line: y = 3x - 2.
Parallel gradient = 3.
Perpendicular gradient = -1/3.
Through (1,4):
parallel y = 3x + 1;
perpendicular y - 4 = -(1/3)(x - 1).
Teaching emphasis: equal gradients for parallel lines; m₁m₂=-1 for non-vertical perpendicular lines.

### P1-COO-04 — Equation of a circle
Example:
centre (2,-1), radius 3
(x-2)² + (y+1)² = 9.
Expanded:
x² + y² - 4x + 2y - 4 = 0.
Teaching emphasis: signs inside brackets are opposite to centre coordinates; complete squares to recover centre/radius from expanded form.

### P1-COO-05 — Lines and circles
Example:
x² + y² = 25
y = 3
⇒ x² + 9 = 25
⇒ x = ±4
⇒ intersections (-4,3), (4,3).
Teaching emphasis: substitute line into circle, solve, then recover/check both coordinates; connect algebraic roots to geometric intersections.

### P1-COO-06 — Intersections, tangency and parameter conditions
Example:
circle x² + y² = 25, horizontal line y = k.
Substitution gives x² + k² - 25 = 0.
As a quadratic in x, tangency means exactly one real solution, so discriminant = 0:
100 - 4k² = 0 ⇒ k = ±5.
Teaching emphasis: two roots = two intersections, one repeated root = tangent, no real roots = no intersection.
