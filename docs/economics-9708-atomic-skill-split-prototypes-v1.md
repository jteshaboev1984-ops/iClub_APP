# Economics 9708 — Atomic Learning-Skill Split Prototypes v1

**2026-10-09 | Stage 1 DRAFT | No academic signoff, no runtime use**

## Why split?
Official Cambridge numeric requirements are **parent contracts** that frequently contain multiple distinct learning performances. A broad card labelled `7.6.4` (all market structures) or `8.1.1` (all interventions) would omit meaningful concepts and exaggerate AI Tutor's coverage. This proposes **45 atomic draft skills across just 5 especially broad parent subpoints**. No claim is made that these 45 are the final or exhaustive count. Nor may they be appended to the 253 official points as a naïve total: the atomic denominator requires **every** parent to be split, merged or kept under common governance rules.

Official syllabus authority: [Cambridge Economics 9708 syllabus for examination in 2026–2028, version 2](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/697423-2026-2028-syllabus.pdf), specific subpoints 7.6.4, 8.1.1, 9.1.1, 10.3.1, 11.3.3; user-supplied *Economics 4th edition* chapters 35, 38, 41, 47 and 50. These *original English outcomes* are editorial proposals, not paraphrased exam questions or Cambridge quotations.

## Critical academic checks before sign-off
1. **One independently diagnostic action per skill**; where quantitative tasks require calculations and interpretation, decide if they are distinct learning states. Preserve reasoning and evaluation as evidence; don't treat all written answers as auto-markable.
2. **7.6.4** requires full market-type comparisons, short/long run and payoff matrices; do not let a single oligopoly MCQ stand for the parent.
3. **8.1.1** all twelve policy classes require clear welfare/implementation conditions; some measures may share theory with earlier AS 3.2, but the A Level evaluation depth is distinct.
4. **9.1.1** cover differing sector leakage assumptions and all named average/marginal propensities, with units and explicit formula assumptions; no AS multiplier mastery.
5. **10.3.1** Laffer curve, alternative policies and multiobjective tradeoffs are independent analytical actions.
6. **11.3.3** money/non-money/composite measures, PPP, HDI, MEW, MPI, Kuznets; do not confuse with distribution/Gini requirement 11.4.2.
7. These provisional keys must stay in private academic mapping, **not displayed as names in iClub**. RU and UZ equivalence, age-appropriate examples and rights checks are required before subsequent stages.
