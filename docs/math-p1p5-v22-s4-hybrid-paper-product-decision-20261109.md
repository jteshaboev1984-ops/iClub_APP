# iClub Mathematics P1/P5 — S4 hybrid full-Paper product decision (architect approved)

**Decision date:** 2026-11-09. **Authority:** architect's explicit chat decision after reviewing three external-platform studies and iClub's existing Paper implementation. **Tracking:** [Issue #450](https://github.com/jteshaboev1984-ops/iClub_APP/issues/450). **Status:** PRODUCT DECISION / DOCUMENTATION ONLY; implementation, migration, deployment and Mathematics Core MASS GO are **NOT** authorized by this document.

This decision **updates the proposed S4 full-Paper learner journey** in the [v2.2 integration roadmap](math-p1p5-v22-main-integration-roadmap-20261109.md) and takes precedence over older P4-02…P4-08 proposals **where those proposals assume that a learner must type/upload their complete written work to complete a paper-training session**. It does not erase prior accepted engineering evidence, rewrite historical paper responses or replace the [P4-08 ordinary written-learning contract](https://github.com/jteshaboev1984-ops/iClub_APP/blob/feature/math-p1p5-p4-08-written-criteria-optional-contract-20261107/docs/math-p1p5-p4-08-independent-written-work-criteria-core-contract-20261107.md).

## Decision in one paragraph

**HYBRID:** a learner attempts a full iClub P1/P5 Paper **on physical paper**, with the existing trusted question bank, server-timed exam-like practice and a saved **training-session** record. Typed multi-step mathematical working, photo/PDF upload, self-awarded marks, AI and Mentor are **not required**. After finishing, but **before the first answer/criteria reveal**, the learner **may** submit a small, independently source-reviewed set of **final numeric/symbolic answer checkpoints**. The service returns *match / no match / not checked* for that **final answer only**, **never Cambridge paper marks**, method marks, percentage grade, or a verified written solution. Review then offers source-approved criteria/explanations and voluntary topic-repeat choices. Core learning remains available without taking a Paper, performing checkpoints, viewing criteria, mentor marking or AI marking.

## 1. Learner workflow (Math P1 and P5 independently)

1. **Choose a source-approved full Paper** by component, paper key and immutable assessment/version. Display the actual content contract, allowed time and maximum *possible* paper marks as **paper metadata**, never as an awarded score. A printable/PDF view is optional **only after its full legal/source/quality preparation has been verified**; no assumed ready PDF assets.
2. **Start** a server-authorized training session. Render complete questions in an exam-friendly view with usable zoom/navigation and an exam-clock computed from persisted server start/deadline. Working happens in the student's own notebook or paper; **no required per-question typed answer, mark input, screenshot, solved checkbox or all-questions-viewed condition**. On return from background/sleep, reconcile the clock with server time. No compulsory minimum dwell time, page-visits quota or invented anti-cheat constraint. On expiry or early exit record the actual state (timed out / ended early / closed normally), not an assertion of successful academic completion.
3. **Finish** with a simple confirmation, preserving true timestamps and paper identity. A completed training session means **learner invoked completion of a rehearsal**; it is NOT evidence that handwritten mathematics was completed, correctly solved, examiner-marked, or independently assessed. Preserve incomplete/interrupted history separately and never rewrite legacy signed timed/paper submissions.
4. **Optional quick final-answer check**, only **before** any protected official solution, marking-criteria content or answer key has been revealed for this attempt. Offer the option to **Skip and see review** without losing access. Only select previously academically reviewed checkpoint items with unambiguous final-answer equivalence, accepted variants/units/rounding and reliable deterministic server verification. Unsupported items have **no input**, no fabricated key and no provisional credit. On revealing solutions, close the *independent checkpoint* window for that attempt (later comparison may be explanatory only, never independent assessed evidence). No guess/answer matching may inflate `L3`, paper marks or grade readiness.
5. **Result:** two separate areas. (A) **Training journal**: which P1/P5 Paper/version, started/finished, recorded elapsed clock, normal/early/expired/interrupted, and optional review flags. (B) **Final-answer checkpoints**: explicit numerator/denominator only across the answered, genuinely independently-checked checkpoints, described as *"final answers matched"* rather than paper score, mathematical-method assessment or percentage of official 75/50 marks. Display **"Written method: not assessed"** and **"Full Paper mark: unknown"** for unmarked writing.
6. **Guided review after exit/expiry**: previously approved, appropriately licensed rubric requirements, worked explanation **only where actually available** and syllabus-skill mapping. Optional learner flag `repeat topic`, `ran out of time`, `review again` may inform **recommendations only**, not correctness, mastery, forced lesson access or scores. Review opening is read-only/noncredit. If the same Paper is reattempted after answers were exposed, explicitly distinguish it from a first blind rehearsal; do not treat it as a fresh independently scored exam.

## 2. Immutable scoring rule — NO student-awarded grades

**BINDING ARCHITECT DECISION: THE LEARNER NEVER GRADES THEMSELF.** No numeric written self-mark controls, M/A/B self-mark fields, question score sliders, "I earned N marks" forms, inferred marks from checked final answers or a self-marking backdoor. Do not re-enable `public.submit_exam_prep_timed_written_self_mark_safe_v1`; D03 firewall and old-client denial remain intact.

Keep distinct, with source/provenance:
- **Rehearsal event** — one person/time/paper session; not verified solution correctness or mastery.
- **Deterministic answer match** — an optional server-confirmed *final-answer-only* datum, never a whole-paper score or method mark. Do not silently feed it into legacy academic mastery, Stage5 readiness or grades.
- **Independently marked written solution** — **future only**; not present by virtue of written evidence/upload or choice of paid tier.

**Future possibilities ONLY (NOT implemented, NOT an access condition, NOT current score sources):**
- **Mentor/qualified human review:** optional when a real authorized reviewer and reviewed source rubric/workflow exist; provenance and reviewed scope must be explicit; no mandatory Mentor Care, forced queue, response-time promise, or human review required for Core.
- **AI review/marking of handwritten workings:** potentially available **only for learners with an eligible tariff/plan**, explicit entitlement and required consent, after the architect separately approves accuracy, Cambridge method-mark validation, privacy, provenance, cost caps, abuse resistance and release. No paid-tier AI workflow or marks are enabled by this decision. If introduced, display **AI evaluation** with its source and limitations, **never silently upgrade an AI suggestion into an externally verified Cambridge/examiner mark**. Access to ordinary Paper practice, checkpoints, review and Core is NOT gated by an AI plan.

Tariff products, entitlement price, reviewer availability, how AI scores are quality-controlled, and whether verified human-marked data can influence formal readiness are **future separate design/approval questions**, not implicitly resolved here.

## 3. Actual content baseline and asset checks

Live source-backed read-only inventory on 2026-11-09: **8 published assessment *versions* per component, but 3 distinct full-Paper keys per component** (P1 and P5). Current latest versions cover **30 P1 task placements / 30 distinct written task IDs** and **21 P5 / 21 distinct written task IDs** across their three Papers. They are **100% written**, with **zero objective items**: do not pretend a ready MCQ or auto-graded checkpoint bank exists.

The live source has assessment question-to-skill mapping, nonempty rubric JSON, published QA-pass task records and approved RU/UZ criteria locales for all current full-Paper task placements. **This does not prove** that every task has a complete accessible worked solution, that printable PDFs exist, that final answers have been independently validated as checkable, or that formula/statistical tables are bundled. Before implementing/checkpoint enablement: audit exact **three current Paper versions per component** (not all archived versions), each item/locale, method-vs-final-answer distinction, explicit accepted final answers and rounding/units, math notation/graphics, worked solution availability and review provenance, copyright/licensing, print/PDF assets, and approved **Cambridge MF19**/normal tables access. **If no checkpoint passes academic QA, show paper-first+review honestly; never force a checker on unsuitable content.**

Official Cambridge past-paper entries in `private.exam_prep_paper_metadata` are currently **metadata-only external links**, not publication rights for mirroring copyrighted PDFs.

## 4. Stage3–6 migration reconciliation: product-rule change, not a silent shortcut

Historical source-only draft work [PR #494](https://github.com/jteshaboev1984-ops/iClub_APP/pull/494), [#506](https://github.com/jteshaboev1984-ops/iClub_APP/pull/506), [#507](https://github.com/jteshaboev1984-ops/iClub_APP/pull/507) proved digital submission/clock/real nonblank response and paper/no-score separation. **Those E2E proofs remain valid for their tested legacy/digital pathway**, but do NOT prove the newly approved no-upload Paper practice can satisfy the old `private.exam_prep_timed_execution_comparable_v1` requirement that real written answer artifacts exist.

Do **NOT** simply remove its answer-integrity guard, fake `answered_items`, mark empty work "verified", or reinterpret 3 paper rehearsal clicks as successful assessed Paper completion. Preserve the old comparator for legacy genuine timed-paper records. Design a **separate non-assessed training-session fact**, reconcile Stage3/4/5/6 operational transitions against the **existing objective academic mastery/correction/retest requirements**, and propose the precise replacement for the prior three "execution-comparable full Paper" condition **for architect approval before writing executable stage promotion SQL**. There must be **no newly imposed required mentor, AI, file upload, checkpoint, minimum duration, grade threshold or learning quota**. App Readiness remains explicit **insufficient/unverified for method and full-paper performance** where independent marks are absent. Preserve P1 and P5 distinctions and existing genuine learner history.

The S4 task is therefore **design-sensitive**, not a mechanical port of all P4-02→P4-08 migrations as-is. Reuse only existing safe source contracts and test coverage where truly compatible. Do not resurrect superseded numeric learner self-marking.
