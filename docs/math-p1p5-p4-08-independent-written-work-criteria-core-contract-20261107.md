## Fixed product rule (do not reinterpret in future work)

1. iClub presents written exercises for P1/P5; the learner solves each problem **independently**, on paper or in their own working environment. **The app does not grade or verify the written mathematical solution**. It does not assign Cambridge-style marks or a correctness percentage, and does not ask for the learner's own numeric self-mark.
2. The existing UX presents a learner-controlled **collapsible marking-criteria panel** for the current written learning task. Opening the criteria is **optional**, for the learner to compare their own solution with the task requirements. The criteria are *requirements*, not a revealed protected answer, awarded score or proof of mastery. Localized RU/UZ/EN; follow the code's accessible button/expanded state, including hide/reopen.
3. The Mathematics Core learning sequence **continues without requiring the criteria to be opened, a typed essay uploaded, an app correctness assessment, AI service or Mentor review**. The existing `Next` workflow records only honest noncredit step completion, never correctness credit. Do not add artificial dates, hour requirements, additional consent or other gates. The earlier prohibition of scored written self-evaluation remains valid: optional *reading criteria* is not *self-grading*.
4. Preserve **two distinct components P1 and P5** and two distinct records: (a) valid Paper/work **execution** (server-authorized, timings, integrity, not blank/whitespace-only) vs. (b) **verified awarded marks** (unknown for ungraded written solutions). Paper execution is not a grade or verified readiness; Stage 6 operational progression may continue under existing objective-evidence conditions, and App Readiness stays `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` when independent written marks are unavailable. Separate objective MCQ/short-answer assessment can still be app-verified by its established deterministic server evaluator.
5. This documentation is **not** an authorization to change currently running users or production. No merge, SQL deployment, Vercel release, feature-enable, content publication or beta cleanup without previously defined explicit GO. Practice, Tours, certificates, learner progress, other subjects and the independent P0-16B safety task are untouched.
