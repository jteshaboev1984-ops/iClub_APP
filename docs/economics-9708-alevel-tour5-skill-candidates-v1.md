## Critical split requirements
- **8.1.1** is particularly broad: at least taxes, subsidies, pollution permits, prices, licences, regulation, information, public ownership/privatisation, behavioural policies. A single card for all intervention instruments would not meet the teaching objective.
- **8.2.5** needs individual policy comparison and evaluation (means testing, universal support, basic income, negative income tax); the existence of redistribution multiple-choice questions is not proof of the full set.
- **8.3.4** is two separate evidence types: calculating MRP and using MRP for a firm's labour-demand curve.
- **8.3.8** requires three different labour-market models (union, national minimum wage, monopsony), with correct diagram, wage and employment effects.
- **8.3.10** (economic rent / transfer earnings) is a known weak spot: **no direct Practice candidate** in chapter 40 despite 25 items. Needs verification against all item banks, not fabricated coverage.
