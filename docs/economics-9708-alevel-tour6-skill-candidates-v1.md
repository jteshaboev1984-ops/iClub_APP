- **9.4.3:** quantity theory MV=PT is explicitly required. Earlier Q2540 is in AS Practice Tour 2 with incorrect tier, while no candidate was selected in the 70 current A Level Practice Tour 6 by its metadata. Check the complete question bank before claiming absence.
- **9.4.7–9.4.8:** liquidity preference and loanable funds Keynesian rate determination not covered by a precise candidate among the current 70; fundamental A Level theory gaps to validate.
- **10.1 vs 10.2:** macro objectives and interactions differ. Much of Ch45 existing Practice is about fiscal policy and inflation rather than its recorded official topic 10.1. These need question-level crosswalk.
- **10.2.5:** both traditional and expectations-augmented Phillips curves required; former AS Ch20 Practice is a known tier anomaly. Do not promote AS mastery from a misplaced question.
- **10.3.1:** fiscal policy needs Laffer curve as well as broader policy comparison; a single policy-mix question is insufficient.
