Corrections applied
1. Question 5968: removed an out-of-source meso-form question. The coursebook teaches that a molecule with two independent chiral centres can have four optical isomers, but does not introduce the meso exception. It was replaced with a source-aligned question on CH3CH(OH)CH(NH2)COOH, which has two chiral centres and four possible optical stereoisomers when internal symmetry is absent.
2. Question 5974: replaced the non-unique claim that dissolving an ionic solid necessarily increases entropy. The revised question uses vaporisation, which unambiguously increases the number of accessible particle and energy arrangements.
3. Question 6006: clarified the acylation explanation. Phenylamine forms N-phenylethanamide, and HCl formed in the substitution is normally neutralised by excess amine or another base.
